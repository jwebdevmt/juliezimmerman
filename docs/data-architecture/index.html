Philosophy

Engineering Philosophy

Data architecture is the discipline of keeping meaning intact across systems, time, and scale.

A correct schema reflects the real business rules, not just the current screen.

  • Imports and migrations need explicit validation and recovery paths.
  • Synchronization should make freshness, ownership, and failure visible.
  • Privacy can often be protected through architecture rather than policy alone.

Approach

How I Work

I trace data from source to consumer before changing it. That includes field semantics, timing, transformations, identifiers, permissions, and the behavior of downstream systems.

I design for partial failure: resumable imports, validation checkpoints, useful logs, and clear distinctions between source truth, derived data, and cached presentation.

Business Value

Why data integrity matters

A pipeline can run successfully while still delivering the wrong meaning. Reliable architecture tests more than transport: it verifies semantics, freshness, completeness, and downstream behavior.

That discipline reduces customer-facing errors, failed migrations, and the hidden cost of reconciling systems after trust has been lost.
