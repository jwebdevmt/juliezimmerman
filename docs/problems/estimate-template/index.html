Engineering Case Study

Turning Ad Hoc Estimates into a Repeatable Process

The Problem

Pricing and scoping were handled ad hoc, without a consistent structure.

Constraints

The format had to work across project sizes and incorporate both development and design concerns.

Approach

I created an estimate template and refined it with the designer as real projects exposed gaps and edge cases.

Outcome

Estimates became faster, more consistent, and cumulative rather than being rebuilt from scratch each time.

Looking Back

The collaborative refinement mattered as much as the original template. Development and design saw different risks, and the estimate became stronger by holding both perspectives.

Perspectives

Julie: The template became institutional learning: every difficult estimate could improve the next one.

Bo Ra: The collaboration preserved two different ways of seeing the same client commitment.

Engineering Principles

  • A useful standard improves through real use rather than pretending to be complete on day one.
